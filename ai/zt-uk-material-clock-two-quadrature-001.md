## Intuition route

Two directional phase encounters do not tell a material clock how to combine them. C2 shows a two-storage bound oscillator can generate the square root as its natural frequency. C3-C5 then remove the tempting shortcuts: ordinary reversal does not swap the two internal quadratures, and ordinary wave/cavity round trips give the harmonic 1-beta^2 result. The unresolved physics is a constitutive matter interaction that couples the two directional slips oppositely to conjugate energy-storage sectors.

## Geometry route

## Mathematical route

C1: competing means demonstrate underdetermination. C2: Omega^2=AB gives the square root conditionally for A=1-beta,B=1+beta. C3: that factorization is covariant under beta reversal only with an additional canonical quadrature exchange, and the same output admits nonunique A,B factorizations. C4/C5: Maxwell/path reversal does not produce that exchange; fixed-length round-trip/cavity closure gives 1-beta^2.

## Logical route

Reject output-driven mean or factorization selection. Reject path-reversal=duality and forward/backward=E/B identifications. The C2 square-root becomes a physical clock law only if a microscopic constitutive directional-storage map is independently derived. v0.26 clarification: the TSPR route can treat (1-beta)(1+beta) as downstream factorization of a squared projection, so C2 is no longer the only plausible source of a square root. This does not invalidate the C2 exact-conditional eigenfrequency; it prevents treating its factorization as uniquely inferred from the output.
