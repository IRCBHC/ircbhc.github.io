## Assumptions

- Clean-room material response is a toy/constraint source, not the electron law.
- Rigid-body S=I omega is a control only.

## Intuition route

Free radial/transverse response prevents a prior kinematic restriction from being mistaken for a proof that every extended electron is impossible; the internal dynamics must then be solved rather than assumed.

## Geometry route

Extended non-rigid internal geometry with radial equilibrium must be derived from electron microphysics; no fixed-radius or fixed-transverse shortcut is allowed.

## Mathematical route

Rigid control: fixed S with I proportional to m r^2 gives v_eq proportional to 1/r. Candidate route must replace the rigid law with a derived phase/field angular-momentum functional and later derive F(Q^2) or equivalent.

## Logical route

Failure of a rigid classical realization does not entail failure of every extended non-rigid realization. Conversely, radial freedom does not entail quantum spin or point-like compatibility.

## Observational route

Spin-1/2, g/magnetic-moment channel, and scattering point-likeness remain downstream targets. Target-input firewall is mandatory.

## Evidence independence assessment

No measured spin, g, radius, compositeness/scattering target is allowed to choose the internal law. Current stage is a connected research route only.

## Important negative results

- radial shrinking alone does not solve the rigid fixed-S equator control
- quantum spin is not to be relabeled ordinary rigid rotation
- point-like readout does not by itself prove zero ontology radius in the project semantics
