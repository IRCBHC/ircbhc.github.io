  • Local Wave Geometry, Operational Units, and CMB Temperature Comparison

    23 November 2026 · Research manuscript v0.9.3

    Develops a local rotating-wave geometry and operational units, then compares a conditional CMB temperature relation with published reconstructions.

  • From Primordial Electron–Positron Pairs to Compact Seeds and Baryonic Matter

    21 November 2026 · Research perspective v0.6

    Proposes a common mechanism linking primordial pair populations, possible compact seeds and proton-like outcomes. It sets out the physical links and experiments needed to test the proposal.

  • When Equations Confirm Their Premises

    20 November 2026 · Research perspective v0.2

    Uses positron-plasma evidence and a source–sink simulation to examine which conclusions depend on model assumptions and which could be tested independently.

  • A Structure-Dependent Route to Cosmic Acceleration in Zero Theory

    20 November 2026 · Preprint v2.1

    Proposes a structure-dependent route to cosmic acceleration. Conditional volume-averaging results and counterexamples show what can be inferred from differences in regional expansion rates.

  • The Lake That Boils Quietly

    19 November 2026 · Illustrated research essay

    Uses a lake, fountains and vortices to explore what an observer can measure and to motivate a question about the constancy of light speed.

  • Electromagnetic Wave Geometry, Maxwell Evolution, and Causal Interpretation

    18 November 2026 · Working paper

    Compares an independent rotating-pair construction with Maxwell evolution in the same wave domain and examines what the equations establish about causal interpretation.
